Workspaces are isolated spaces for your notes to help you separate your notes by concern, project, area of work, or similar. For example, you can create a "My Project" workspace to keep your work related notes in, and a "Personal life" workspace to keep your diaries in.


When you launch Darkwrite for the first time, you will choose a name for your first workspace. Every note must belong to a workspace, this is why we initialize a default one for you on first launch.

## Create a new workspace

Using the workspace switcher at the bottom right of the window, at the end of the sidebar, you can create a new workspace by choosing "Create new workspace".

A dialog will ask for a name for the new workspace. **You can always change it later in settings.**

## Switching to a workspace
Choose the workspace from the switcher. It will immediately switch on click.

## Edit a workspace

![Edit workspace dialog](/img-docs/edit-workspace-dialog.webp)

Click the settings icon next to the workspace switcher. In the "Workspace" tab, you'll see an option to "Edit workspace". When you click it, a dialog will appear as shown in the image.

You can choose any image as the new icon for your workspace. Cropping your images to a square shape beforehand is recommended to avoid weird looks.


## Delete a workspace
![Edit workspace dialog](/img-docs/delete-workspace.webp)

First, switch to the workspace you want to delete. Then open settings, and choose "Delete workspace". You will be prompted to re-enter the workspace's name to confirm you are doing this consciously. 

<Aside type="danger">
Once you delete a workspace, all notes associated with that workspace will be deleted permanently, with no way to recover them. Your notes **WILL NOT** end up in the recycle bin. **They will be gone forever.** 

If there are some notes you want to keep, make sure to export them as JSON first so you can import them back to a different workspace.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">
If "Delete workspace" button in workspace settings is disabled, this means you are trying to delete your final remaining workspace. If you want to delete it, create a new workspace first, then delete the other workspace.
</Aside>
